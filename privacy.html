Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Marketing Solutions with Az'Ha ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us: when you fill in our contact form we receive your name, email address, phone number and monthly ad budget range. If you message us on WhatsApp or book a call, we also receive the details you share there.
  • Booking details: when you book a strategy call through Calendly, Calendly collects your name, email and chosen time slot and shares them with us.
  • Automatic information: like most websites, we and our service providers collect technical data such as your browser, device, approximate location, pages visited and how you arrived at the site. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and schedule or hold the strategy call you asked for.
  • To understand how visitors use the site and to improve it.
  • To measure and improve our own advertising, including showing our ads to people who visited this site.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam.

Cookies, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to see how the site is used, and the Meta Pixel to measure the results of our Facebook and Instagram ads and to show our ads to people who have visited the site. These tools use cookies and may collect your IP address, device information and the pages or buttons you interact with.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and control the ads you see on Facebook and Instagram in your Meta ad preferences.

Third-party services

We rely on other companies to run the site. They process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Formspree, which delivers our contact form submissions to us.
  • Calendly, for booking calls.
  • Google (Analytics and Google Fonts) and Meta (Pixel, Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp).
  • Font Awesome, loaded from a content delivery network, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

When you follow a link to Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp, you leave this site and their policies apply.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above as needed to run the site and respond to you, or when the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as we need them to respond to you and provide our services, and then delete them or keep them only in a form that doesn't identify you. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of the tools we use.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you at any time. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we can.

Children

This site is intended for business owners and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy
